Headline index in preparation

A second series, weighted with CyStat's Household Budget Survey shares so it is directly comparable to the official Consumer Price Index for food and non-alcoholic beverages. It uses Jevons elementary aggregates over unit prices within each COICOP class. It publishes monthly once the product classification is complete.

Comparison series: CyStat CPI, ECOICOP 2 classification, 2025 = 100.

Method in brief

  • Unit prices only. Everything is €/kg, €/L or €/piece, so a pack that shrinks at the same shelf price reads as inflation.
  • Fixed basket. Nothing enters or leaves for twelve months. Re-based each January and chain-linked.
  • Paid price is the headline. Promotional prices open to everyone count, as in the official CPI. Shelf prices and the promotional gap are published alongside.
  • Churn is imputed, not dropped. A delisted or out-of-stock line takes its group's movement. Last prices are never carried forward silently.
  • Geometric means. Within a class, items are combined with the Jevons formula, not by averaging prices.

Data

Prices are collected once a day from Alphamega's public online catalogue. Branch differences are stock, not price. Everything shown here is reproducible from the published files.
